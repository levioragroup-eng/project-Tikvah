How to Play

Move — WASD / arrow keys, or the joystick on your phone.
Interact — E, or tap the E button. 👋 wave with 1 · ❤️ heart with 2.
The rhythm — each day: 🌾 tend the farm · 🎣 catch a fish · 🍲 cook a meal · 👋 greet a neighbor · 🕯️ light a candle — and the Garden of Hope will bloom 🌸
